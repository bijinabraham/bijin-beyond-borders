Concept C

Coordinate Mark

GPS coordinates as brand identity — precise, modern, tells a story. 28°36'N 77°12'E are New Delhi's coordinates. Could rotate with each story. The icon is the location itself.

Concept D

Flight Path

A curved arc between two points — departure and arrival. Universal travel shorthand. The arc doubles as a horizon line, the dots as cities. Clean enough to be a favicon, meaningful enough to tell the story.

Concept E

Triple B Block

B · B · B — Bijin Beyond Borders as three bold initials in a tight block, separated by dots. The full name is encoded in three letters. Confident, structured, no image required. Works at any size.
