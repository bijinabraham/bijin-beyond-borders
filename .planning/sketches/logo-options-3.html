Postmark

Concept I

Diagonal Split

Two worlds, one frame — the diagonal cut is the border being crossed. Oxblood and terracotta are the two sides. Bold, graphic, instantly distinctive. Works as an app icon, a stamp, a sticker.

Pin Drop
