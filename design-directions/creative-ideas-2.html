Idea 01

Photo Cursor Trail

As the cursor moves across the hero or Travels section, a trail of your travel photos fans out behind it — each image slightly rotated, fading as it ages. Feels like rifling through a stack of prints. Pure CSS + JS, no library needed. Move your mouse below.

Where it lives
Hero section or PlacesGrid — activate on mousemove only in that zone
Effort
~1.5 hr — pool of 8–10 div elements cycled with JS
Impact
Showstopper — nothing else on personal travel sites does this

Idea 02

Scrolling Marquee Strips

Editorial-style ticker strips between sections. Two lanes running in opposite directions — one lists countries visited, the other lists activities. Hover to pause. Adds rhythm and motion without JS. Framer Motion or pure CSS.

Idea 04

Film Grain Texture Overlay

A single fixed SVG noise layer sitting over the entire page at low opacity. Gives the warm off-white palette a physical, tactile quality — like heavy paper. Left half: no grain. Right half: grain active. One line of CSS, zero JS.

Without grain
With grain
Where it lives
Single ::after on body — covers everything, no component changes
Effort
~10 min — add 8 lines of CSS to globals.css
Impact
Medium — subtle but makes the whole site feel more considered and handcrafted

Idea 05

Magnetic Buttons

Primary buttons physically move toward the cursor as it approaches — up to ~20px pull. When the cursor leaves the button's orbit, it snaps back with a spring. Ghost links get a subtler pull. Move your cursor near the buttons below.

Move cursor near the buttons

Where it lives
Hero CTA "Enter", footer contact link, any primary button
Effort
~1 hr — reusable MagneticBtn wrapper component
Impact
High — feels premium and tactile on desktop, degrades gracefully on touch
