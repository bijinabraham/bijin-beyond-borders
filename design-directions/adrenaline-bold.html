Idea 01 · POV Flash

Become The Sport — First-Person Flash

Hold any tab for 800ms and the page becomes your first-person view of the sport. Ski goggles framing a mountain view. Snorkel mask underwater. Boxing ring POV with ropes and lights. Surfboard nose entering frame with the wave horizon. 2-second cinematic moment, then back to the page.

Idea 02 · Cursor Transformation

The Cursor IS The Gear

Your cursor isn't a dot. It's a tiny boxing glove on MMA. A ski boot on Ski. A surfboard outline on Surf. A flipper on Snorkel. Hand-drawn SVG silhouettes, ~32px. Subtle but immediately tells you what sport you're in. Hover the demo and click tabs.

MMA
Effort
~2 hr — design 6 small SVG cursors, swap based on active activity. Falls back to standard cursor on horizon items.
Impact
Memorable. Sells the sport identity instantly. Pure signature detail.

Idea 03 · Title Physics

The Name Behaves Like The Sport (Always)

Not on hover. Constantly. The activity name has its own physics simulation per sport: MMA letters jolt from impact randomly. SURF letters ride a wave. SKI letters slide diagonally. SNORKEL letters drift gently. The name is a tiny living thing.

All animations are constant. No hover needed.
Per-sport behaviour
MMA: random punch jolts · SKI: diagonal carve drift · SURF: sine wave bob · SNORKEL: slow float · SKYDIVE: gravity drop with bounce-back
Why bold
The name itself becomes the show. No hover required — it's alive the whole time you're on the page.

Idea 04 · Switch Storm

Page-Wide Storm on Switch

Switching activities triggers a 1-second weather event across the entire page. Switch to MMA: red impact shockwave + screen shake. SKI: snow blizzard sweeps left-to-right. SURF: water spray crashes from edge. SNORKEL: bubble explosion. SKYDIVE: cloud rush. SCUBA: ink darkness.

MMA
Duration
~1.2 seconds. Long enough to feel cinematic, short enough to not annoy on rapid switching.
Impact
Every tab click is a moment. You feel each transition. The page reacts physically.

Idea 06 · Page Tilt

Page Physically Tilts With Each Sport

Switching activities subtly tilts the entire content as if the page itself is leaning into the sport. SKI tilts down-right (carving). SURF tilts side-to-side (paddling). SKYDIVE tilts back (free-fall stance). Subtle (1-2 degrees). Felt, not seen. Click each tab below.
