Idea 01

Click a City → Sidebar Becomes a Photo

Clicking any city dot on the globe rotates the globe to center that city and transforms the sidebar into a photo panel — your real photo from that trip, the visit date, and stats. Click a city below.

Idea 02

Animated Flight Arcs Between Trip Cities

Each trip gets a colour-coded great-circle arc connecting its cities in order, with an animated dashed stroke that moves in the direction of travel. Toggle by trip to watch your routes light up.

Japan · Europe trips
US · Canada trips
How it works
D3 geoInterpolate draws great-circle arcs between consecutive cities per trip. CSS dash-offset animates direction
Effort
~1.5 hr — add arc data alongside existing city data, render in the D3 init
Impact
High — your globe goes from scattered dots to a map of journeys

Idea 03

Polaroid Photo Card on City Hover

Instead of a text label tooltip, hovering a city that has real photos shows a polaroid-style photo card — slightly rotated, with the city name handwritten below. Cities without photos keep the existing text label. Move your cursor over the dots below.

Hover the city dots

How it works
On city hover, show an absolutely-positioned div with your real photo. Cities without photos keep the existing text tooltip.
Effort
~45 min — replace text label with photo div in the D3 city group setup
Impact
Medium — beautiful detail, especially once most cities have real photos
