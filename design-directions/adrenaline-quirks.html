Quirk 05

Hold-to-Engage Activity Tabs

Instead of clicking a tab, hold it down for a beat. A charge bar fills, then snaps into the activity with a brief flash. Feels physical, like cocking a weapon or pulling a parachute cord. Hold any button below for ~600ms.

HOLD →
Effort
~45 min — pointer events with charge tracker, fallback to click for accessibility
Caveat
Quick-click still works (after 100ms threshold). Hold is the discoverable upgrade.
Impact
Quirky — divisive (slows down casual users). Could be optional with a click-only fallback

Quirk 06

Idle Glitch Pulse

Every 10-15 seconds while idle, the activity name briefly glitches (RGB-split, slight horizontal shift) before snapping back. Tiny detail. Makes the page feel alive even when no one's interacting. Watch for ~10s.
