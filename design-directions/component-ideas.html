Idea 04

Cursor — Magnetic + Context-Aware

The existing cursor is a dot + lagging ring. 21st.dev's Magnetic Cursor pattern adds context awareness: the ring expands on hover, morphs on buttons (larger fill), and shows a text label (like "View →") when lingering over project cards. Move your mouse in the demo below.
